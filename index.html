About Me

I'm Janina, but I go by Nina online. I draw anime illustrations and manhwa-style pages, and I love expressive characters, soft lighting and cinematic panel framing.

Every piece starts as a rough thumbnail sketch. From there I move on to clean line work, then color, shading and a final polish.
